Initial Consultation

Your first session is a free 15-minute introduction and assessment. We’ll discuss your current diabetes management, goals, and how JABS can support you. This is a chance to ask questions and see if coaching is right for you. JABS is all about understanding your needs and providing personalised support. Thus, the initial consultation is free to ensure you feel comfortable before committing to any paid services.

Individual Support

Individual support includes either a one-off one-on-one coaching session at £24.99 or ongoing monthly WhatsApp support at £19.99/month. These can be purchased seperately depending on your needs and goals. Coaching sessions last 45 minutes and are tailored to your particular needs. We’ll cover blood sugar management, nutrition, lifestyle changes, and practical strategies. Sessions can be held virtually or in-person depending on your location. WhatsApp support provides direct access to your coach for questions, advice, and encouragement between sessions. This is ideal for day-to-day challenges and quick check-ins. You can combine both services for comprehensive support, or choose one based on your preferences. Each option is designed to provide flexible, personalised support to help you manage your diabetes effectively.
